Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 September 2026

Brick & Brew is a private crew app for friends training for a triathlon. It is not a public social network.

Who we are

Brick & Brew is provided by the app developer who invited you to TestFlight. Contact them through App Store Connect / TestFlight if you have questions about this policy.

Data we collect

  • Account. Sign in with Apple gives us an opaque user identifier and, if you share it, your name. We store a display name you choose so teammates can recognize you on the leaderboard.
  • Crew membership. Invite code and team name so you can share one private board.
  • Training. When you connect Strava, we read activity summaries (sport, start time, distance, moving time, elevation, average and max heart rate). We do not post to Strava and we do not download heart-rate streams.
  • Beers. Counts, timestamps, and optional notes you enter in the app.
  • Technical. Strava tokens live in the iOS Keychain. Crew records live in Apple CloudKit.

Why we collect it

We use this data only to sign you in, sync your training, let you log beers, and show the crew leaderboard. We do not sell data, run ads, or track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Where it is stored

Strava tokens stay on-device. Profiles, activities, beers, and the team record are stored in CloudKit (public database, filtered by crew invite code). That is a small private-crew design, not bank-level isolation.

The Cloudflare Worker used for Strava OAuth exchanges a one-time code or refresh token with Strava. It does not keep an athlete database.

Sharing

We share data with Apple (Sign in with Apple, CloudKit, TestFlight) and, if you connect it, Strava. We do not share the crew board with advertisers.

Retention and choices

Disconnect Strava in the Me tab to delete tokens from Keychain. Sign out removes the local session. Email the developer from the Apple ID you used to sign in to request deletion of CloudKit records. Skipping Strava and logging beers only is supported.

Children

The app is intended for adults on a private TestFlight crew and is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Open a TestFlight feedback ticket or email the developer listed on the App Store Connect record for Brick & Brew.
